

Super simple install, I removed the battery and the battery installation plate (where the battery sits). I had to remove anyway to get access to one of the trans mounts. Once removed, I had clear view of both cables. Just a question of removing the old bushings with a prybar and putting in the new ones. I think the hardest part of the job was installing the retainer clip as you had to almost hammer it in. 

After driving a bit with it, the main advantage of this mod is having a LOT less play and jiggle in the shifter. Like, jiggling the shifter in a gear or in neutral, you'll find there's alot less play which is good.
