I'm looking on getting a helmet first because that's kinda the coolest gear piece. These are the one's that I'm currently looking at.

> [!NOTE] Personal Requirements
> I'm very picky in terms of looks and so this is what I'm generally looking for :
> - Full Face/Modular
> - Sports helmet (not MX or dirt)
> - ECE 22.06 or Snell
> - Budget of 600-700$
> - No Color or very little

## Ride509
---

On god they make some of the best looking helmets out there. Eyes are mostly on the [Mach V](https://ride509.com/collections/street-helmets/products/mach-v-helmet?variant=40193037467733) or the [Mach IV Mod](https://ride509.com/products/mach-iv-mod-helmet?variant=40805828722773). Im not considering the commander since apparently the cardo system is mid and I'd rather install comms myself.
